the 5.x/6.x line and republished under a different npm package
(`@fortawesome/fontawesome-free`), which is why this coordinate stopped receiving updates.
Modernising means changing coordinates and updating icon class names in the Thymeleaf
templates — a real migration, not a version bump. Flagged for awareness only.
